Hello, I’m Fajar Siddiq.Player, coach, judge, designer and community builder

I’m a Singaporean yo-yo champion, born and raised in Yishun and now based in Malaysia as a digital nomad. My yo-yo journey began in 1998 during the Hyper Russell Yo-Yo, Henrys and THP era. A regional contest champion, I won my first Malaysian competition at Toyzbar in 2000 and represented Duncan Toys professionally from 2002 to 2014.

I placed 2nd in the X-Division (Offstring) at the 2003 Asia Pacific Yo-Yo Championships and 32nd in the 4A Division preliminaries at the 2015 World Yo-Yo Contest. I’m a pioneer of Singapore’s off-string 4A and freehand 5A divisions, and I continue to mentor players, judge competitions and perform.

In 2005 I founded Dement Clothing by Euphorya, carrying my “Made in Yishun” spirit and “Fight for Passion!” slogan. I design official gear and branding for the Malaysia Yo-Yo Association (MYYA), and my projects include Throw Clothing and the Dement Yo-Yo Cup in Kuala Lumpur. Dement brings together a community of over 100 people across Southeast Asia through yo-yoing, spintop, diabolo, jitter ring, kendama, running, streetwear modelling, action and extreme sports.

Career TimelineMilestones from 1998 to 2026, drawn from the competition record.

1998

First recorded contest: Hyper Russell Yo-Yo Nationals, Singapore. 4th place.

2000

Champion at the BP KillerBee Freestyle in Singapore, and at the Toyzbar Competition in Malaysia (X Division and Shoot the Moon Contest).

2003

First Asia Pacific Yo-Yo Championships podium: X Division, 2nd place.

2008

Champion of the Best Trick Contest at Speed Challenge × Central Yo-Yo Malaysia.

2012

2nd at the Bali International Yo-Yo Open (4A) and 1st in the Long Sleeper Yo-Yo Contest, Central Malaysia, with a 9min 44secs sleep.

2013

Champion, 4A Division, Singapore West Coast Classics.

2015

Competed at the World Yo-Yo Contest in Japan, finishing 32nd in the prelims.

2020

Champion in 5A at the Malaysia Central Regional Yo-Yo Contest, plus 3rd in 2A, 3A and 4A.

2025

Malaysia Yo-Yo Championships: 2nd in 4A and 3rd in 2A.

2026 and on

Still playing, coaching, designing and building the community.
